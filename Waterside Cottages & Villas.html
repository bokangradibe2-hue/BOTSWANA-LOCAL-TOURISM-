      About us

      Waterside Cottages & Villas is located in Notwane, Gaborone, Botswana, alongside the banks of the Notwane Dam on a 4 hectare piece of unspoiled African Bush.

      It consists of nine very private, fully equipped self catering cottages of varying sizes and varying proximity from each other and can comfortably accommodate up to 26 guests at any one given time.  Our accommodation is perfectly suited for families, couples or groups of friends looking for a luxurious get away, tourists who are breaking their trip on route to or on their way back from Northern Botswana or businessmen / women who are tired of cramped  and noisy apartment / hotel environments.

      Waterside Cottages & Villas is not a party venue and does not permit loud music or raucous behaviour. We classify ourselves as a retreat where one can relax and unwind in a beautiful environment.

      Our cottages are a mere 13km from Gaborone CBD towards Lobatse, yet if feels like you are in the middle of nowhere where one can put to use our motto
      'together is our favourite place to be'

      Our Policies

      Should you decide that you would like to make a booking with us please be so kind as to adhere to the following policies in order to ensure that not only you enjoy your stay with us but the other guests on the property do as well:

      Waterside Cottages is not a function, event or party venue and we do not permit the hosting of any form of party even if the whole establishment has been booked by one group
      -
      We do not accept the delivery of surprise gifts/cakes/balloons etc. Nor do we allow external decor companies to come and decorate the room, setup picnics, dinner tables or cater
      -
      No person can operate any speakers, amplifiers, music from a vehicle or any musical instrument during their stay
      -
      Raucous and/or loud behaviour in not accepted
      -
      Only the number of guests booked to stay are permitted on the property, therefore no day visitors are permitted unless by arrangement with management
      -
      Certain other activities are permitted only by arrangement with management
      -
      We do not offer private viewings of the property, the images on this website are 100% accurate
      -
      Persons under the age of 21 must be accompanied by an adult
      -
      No pets are allowed
