Thamalakane River Lodge is ideally located an easy 20-minute drive from Maun Airport en route to the famous Moremi Game Reserve. It is a secluded haven of tranquility nestled in a grove of mature riverine trees on the bank of the Thamalakane River. The rambling lodge has an idyllic setting and is committed to service excellence. It is a delightful place to come home to after a day spent exploring.

Brian M wrote a review Dec 2025
This lodge is a health risk. Full stop.
This lodge is a health risk. Full stop.

We knowingly booked a room without air-conditioning, which was not an issue and fully expected. What was not disclosed is that the rooms have no window screens. That leaves you with two options in December heat: keep the windows closed and bake, or open them and let mosquitoes pour in. There is no third option.

We were forced to leave the windows partially open just to breathe. The mosquito net over the bed was completely inadequate—it does not seal to the floor and does not fully close. As a result, between my wife and me, we woke up with well over 60 mosquito bites in a single night.

This is not just uncomfortable. In a region with malaria risk, this is unacceptable and dangerous.

We stayed here for two nights at the beginning of December and had planned to return for one more night at the end of our trip. After explaining the situation and our health concerns, we asked to cancel the final night. The lodge refused and would not refund the money.

For context, we traveled extensively throughout Botswana on this trip and had zero mosquito issues anywhere else. This problem was isolated to this property due to poor room design and inadequate mosquito protection.

If you value your health, do not stay here. The mosquitoes will eat you alive. Book somewhere else.

Excellent waterfront lodges with views
These traditional design lodges located by river of same name. We had lodges facing the water and you can see variety of wildlife around here including hippos, wetland birds. Amazing sunset photos with water reflections can be seen - see my images. Friendly staff, great service and good food at the restaurant. Quite variable terrain around hotel but paved pathways make getting about easier. There is a swimming pool. International and local power sockets available in room - see photo. Preferably drink bottled water and keep extra water. Take malaria tablets as malaria is known to be around. There's ceiling fan in the room which we used 24/7 as it was hot during day especially.
Insider tip:
Summer times its hot. Book water facing lodges. Take insect repellent and malaria tablets. Drink bottle water

Seaside22313791264 wrote a review Aug 2025
Avoid complet overpriced Makoro excursion!!!
We stayed at Talamakane Lodge and booked a Makoro boat trip combined with a bush walk for 2 adults and 2 children. The price was a shocking USD 800 (!!!) – BY FAR the most expensive excursion of our entire Zimbabwe and Botswana trip.

To be fair: everything was well organized, the Makoro polers were extremely kind and did their job with great dedication, and two English-speaking guides accompanied us and explained the surroundings. They really tried their best, but the bush walk took place during midday, when hardly any animals are active. For safety reasons, animals were seen only from a great distance, so we mainly observed tracks and footprints rather than wildlife itself.

The lunch provided was decent, but what shocked us most: the guides and Makoro polers received no food at all!!! Out of pity, the group shared part of our meal with them, and they were visibly grateful as they were clearly hungry. At the end, we gave a small tip – but since the trip was already outrageously overpriced, we kept it modest. The polers looked very disappointed, which left us with the uncomfortable impression that they rely on tips because they are poorly paid by the organizing agency.

This experience left us with a very bad feeling. It seems obvious that the Makoro drivers and guides are being exploited by the tour operator, while tourists are being massively overcharged. We strongly advise against booking this activity. During our trip we had far better and more authentic full-day excursions elsewhere – at just a fraction of this price.

In short: The Makoro polers are wonderful people, but the agency behind this tour is taking advantage of both them and the tourists.

Avoid this trip – it’s a complete rip-off! The only really bad experience we had of our trips to South Africa, Zimbabwe and Botswana.
