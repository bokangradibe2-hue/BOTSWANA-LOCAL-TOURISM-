Okavango Delta Ecotourism Camps & Safari Lodges

The Okavango Delta is at the forefront of Ecotourism safari lodges and camps in Botswana & Africa. For a remote, exclusive and eco-friendly safari lodge experience, visit the Okavango Delta.

Spend your nights in the comfort of our hand picked selection of eco-friendly safari lodges and camps in the Okavango Delta. The Botswana Ecotourism Certification System is designed to encourage and support responsible tourism businesses and make sure they provide a quality eco-friendly product to consumers.

With safari lodges and camps in the Okavango Delta being some of the 23 hotels and lodges being certified as eco friendly in Botswana, the Okavango is a fantastic destination if you are looking for ecotourism accommodation on your African safari. Keep an eye out for the Eco Tourism Certified logo on our Okavango Delta safari lodge selection.

Unforgettable Okavango Delta Experiences

  • Soar Over Pools & Lagoons

    Scenic Helicopter Flight
    Scenic helicopter flights over the Okavango Delta’s patchwork landscape of islands, plains and lagoons perfectly illustrate its raw and spectacular beauty.

  • Explore The Tranquil Waterways Of The Delta

    Boat Cruise
    Explore the Okavango Delta’s intricate network of winding channels and glassy lagoons on an Okavango Delta boat cruise safari.

  • Glide Across The Still Waters Of The Delta

    Mokoro Experience
    Glide through a maze of clear channels & opaque lagoons on traditionally made mokoro boats, once carved out of ancient Delta trees.

  • Catch & Release In Serene Aquatic Surroundings

    Fishing Safari
    Catch and release in serene aquatic surroundings on an Okavango Delta fishing safari. The Okavango is renowned as one of Africa’s top fishing destinations.

  • Walk The Watery Wilderness

    Walking Safari
    Let nature be your guide as you embark on a walking safari through this water wilderness known as the Okavango Delta. Ecotourism Okavango Delta walking safari.

  • Go In Search Of The Delta’s Rich Wildlife

    Safari Game Drive
    Go in search of the Okavango Delta’s rich wildlife. Explore a world of water and wildlife on an Okavango Delta game drive. Enjoy Ecotourism in the Delta.
