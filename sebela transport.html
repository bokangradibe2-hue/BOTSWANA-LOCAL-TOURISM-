Travel Bookings
Seabelo Express provides travellers with an added feature. Passengers who intend on travelling into South Africa may purchase tickets at Seabelo Reservations office located in Gaborone West. Seabelo Express does not currently have a route to Johannesburg or elsewhere across the border, but travellers can pre-purchase their tickets in Botswana. Seabelo Express collaborates with Greyhound (South Africa) a leading transport provider in South Africa, which provides services to over 100 destinations across the nation.
For further information about Greyhound services and travelling in South Africa log on to: www.greyhound.co.za
Seabelo Group is recently researching various strategies for a booking system locally in Botswana and internationally, as and when a new service will be established, all necessary information will be updated into the website.
