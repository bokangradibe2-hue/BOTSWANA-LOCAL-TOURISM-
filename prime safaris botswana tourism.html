Main Tourism Attractions in Botswana, Unique Things to See in Botswana—What to See in Botswana

What is Botswana known for? Wonderful attractions in Botswana make it an outstanding tourist destination among the safari countries in Southern Africa.

This country holds one of the most amazing wilderness areas on Earth, featuring a pristine landscape as well as a rich diversity of African wildlife. The country is a prime site for holidaymakers with an interest in incredible sightings of typical safari animals!

Travelers on Botswana safaris have a great opportunity to explore Botswana's tourism attractions. Among the main attractions of Botswana are wonderful game animals, including all the Big Five: hippos, zebras, giraffes, cheetahs, endangered wild dogs, and several plains game animals.

On your Botswana safari tours, the best areas to encounter the above animals include Chobe National Park, Okavango Delta, Nxai Pan National Park, Moremi Wildlife Reserve and Moremi Game Reserve, as well as Kalahari Desert, known for its incredible black-manned Lions and the most endangered species in the desert like Meerkats, Brown Hyenas, etc.

Among the other amazing attractions found in Botswana is the Makgadikgadi Salt Pans. They are stunning salt pans known to be the largest salt pans on Earth!

There is always something unique for travelers undertaking safaris in Botswana, whether they are first-time travelers to Botswana or travelers who have been in this country before. Adventurous wildlife enthusiasts can try out self-drive safari tours!

Best Botswana Tourism Attractions: What to See in Botswana?

Travelers on safari tours in Botswana will have the opportunity to explore various tourism attractions within Botswana found in several safari destinations.

Here are the top attractions of Botswana to explore on your Botswana tours:

  1. Main Botswana Tourism Attractions: Game Animals

Game animals are the famous tourism attractions within Botswana. Within this vast, scenic country, travelers can spot all the game animals tourists on African safaris are usually interested in.

Some of the common game animals in Botswana to look for in their scenic natural habitat are:

  • Lions
  • Leopards
  • Elephants
  • Cheetahs
  • Cape Buffalos
  • Hippos
  • Giraffes
  • Zebras
  • Black and White Rhinos
  • Hyenas
  • Oryx
  • Meer cats and
  • Several Antelope species

The best spots to see game animals in Botswana are:

i). CHOBE NATIONAL PARK,

Situated within northern Botswana, Chobe National Park is among the most amazing parks in Africa. It’s one of the most beautiful places in Botswana due to its incredible wildlife viewing safaris and game drives.

Chobe National Park was named after the Chobe River ecosystem and it’s popularly known for its incredibly big Elephant herds as well as Hippos. Other game animals in Chobe National Park are Lions, Leopards, Cape Buffalos, Cheetahs, Wild dogs, Hyenas, Zebras, Giraffes, and baboons, plus various plains game animals.

The most popular safari activities in Chobe National Park include guided game driving, boat cruises on the Chobe River, and guided walking safaris. Taking a walking safari yields a more rewarding safari experience. Self-drive safari tours are possible here!

On your Botswana trip in Chobe National Park, never miss taking a boat cruise on Chobe River, as it allows you to meet animals like Elephants up close on the banks of the river.

The best time to visit in Botswana Chobe National Park is often in the dry season around May–October. You’ll find several accommodation options, including tented camps of all classes.

ii). CENTRAL KALAHARI GAME RESERVE

Best tourist spots in Botswana! Tourists interested in African wildlife viewing tours (game drives) in Botswana shouldn’t miss visiting Central Kalahari Game Reserve.

It’s a beautiful, extensive protected area situated in the Kalahari wilderness of Botswana, covering about 52,800km2- km2—bigger than the Netherlands—and about 10% of Botswana’s land area.

The Central Kalahari Game Reserve is home to hundreds of game animals; however, the most common are: African bush Elephant, Giraffes, Meerkats, leopards, Lions, Cheetahs, African wild dogs, Plains Zebras, Common Elands, Hartebeests, Greater Kudus, Sable antelope, Black-backed Jackal, Blue wildebeests, Gemsbok, Springbok, Honey badgers, etc. Ostriches and other savannah bird species are common as well.

Never end your game watching safaris in Botswana Central Kalahari Game Reserve without searching for the black-maned Kalahari Lions and enjoying a cultural tour with the San Bushmen.

iii). MOREMI GAME RESERVE : MOREMI WILDLIFE RESERVE

Found in the Eastern part of the Okavango Delta, Moremi Game Reserve is a scenic site for game viewing tours in Botswana, mostly game drives.

While on game drives in Wildlife Reserve Moremi, you can spot all the big five (Lions, Cape Buffalos, Elephants, Leopards, and Rhinos), Hippos, Giraffes, Cheetahs, Hyenas, Nile crocodiles, Greater Kudus, Plain Zebras, Sable Antelopes, Blue Wildebeests, Warthogs, as well as Baboons, etc.

The rare species to look for in Moremi Game Reserve include the black-maned Kalahari Lion, Puku and Red Lechwe Antelope, Sitatunga, Brown Hyena, and Aardvark.

July to October offer the best time for game viewing tours (game drives) in Botswana Moremi Game Reserve, as game animals are easily spotted around water holes. Guided walking safaris are possible in this park.

iv). KHAMA RHINO SANCTUARY

Khama Rhino Sanctuary is also one of the incredible wildlife viewing spots in Botswana. Mainly, it was set to save Botswana’s endangered Rhinos. Therefore, Khama Rhino Sanctuary plays a vital role in Rhino conservation.

This Rhino wildlife sanctuary- (Khama Rhino Sanctuary), about 25km outside Serowe, was established to conserve both the endangered Black and White Rhinos and other animals like Wild dogs, Caracals, leopards, Cheetahs, cheetahs, and elephants.

Currently, this beautiful wildlife sanctuary is home to roughly 40 Rhinos and over 30 other animals, as well as 230 bird species.

Some of the activities in Khama Rhino Sanctuary include guided walking safaris, game drives, and on-foot Rhino tracking.

v). KGALAGADI TRANSFRONTIER PARK

Holding scenic sand dunes and one of the largest salt pans on Earth, Kgalagadi Transfrontier Park is also among the top game viewing sites in Botswana during the rainy summer months- (November–April). It’s great for game drives.

Note that it’s quite challenging to get to Kgalagadi Transfrontier Park, a scenic and vast wilderness (14,670km2) from Botswana! You will need a 4×4 safari vehicle and the ability to camp self-sufficiently. Usually, it’s not accessed from Botswana side.

Kgalagadi Transfrontier Park covers 2 previously separate national parks: the Kalahari Gemsbok National Park of South Africa & Gemsbok National Park of Botswana.

Don’t expect to spot all the Big Five; however, the breathtaking sights of the migrating herds of Wildebeests and Antelopes that attract numerous predators and raptors will leave you with a lifetime safari experience.

vi). NXAI PAN NATIONAL PARK

Situated in north-eastern Botswana side, Nxai Pan National Park is home to abundant wildlife and the untamed terrain of Botswana. It consists of Nxai Pan, which is one of Makgadikgadi Pan salt flats.

Apart from holding wonderful game animals, Nxai Pan National Park also holds a cluster of millennia-old towering baobab trees. Key game animals in Nxai Pan National Park commonly spotted on game drives include Giraffes, Zebras, Wildebeests, Leopards, Lions, Cheetahs, Springbok, Kudu, Impalas, Honey badgers, ostriches, etc.

Note that Nxai Pan National Park is just north of the Maun-Nata main road and it adjoins Makgadikgadi Pans National Park on its northern border.

  1. Best Botswana Tourist Attractions: Okavango Delta, a UNESCO World Heritage Site

The Okavango Delta (the largest inland delta on Earth) ranks among the most commonly visited tourist attractions found in Botswana by travelers interested in African wildlife viewing safaris in Botswana.

Covering roughly 15,000 km2, the Okavango Delta is a UNESCO World Heritage Site in the northwest of Botswana, lying between Moremi Game Reserve. It’s the world’s largest inland delta. You’ll also enjoy the spectacular scenery of the meandering Boteti River.

Unlike other deltas, the flood water of the Okavango Delta (Okavango River) spans out on land, not water; thus, this makes it a purely unique destination to explore. The area is ideal for bird-watching tours and game drives. You’ll enjoy stunning views as the Okavango River cuts through the vast desert.

Some of the notable wild animals spotted on game drives in the Okavango Delta of the Okavango River include lions, witness vast herds of elephants, Cape buffalos, leopards, cheetahs, black and white rhinos, zebras, wildebeests, endangered wild dogs, and giraffes.

The best time for wildlife viewing in the Okavango Delta is during the dry months of May–October. It’s best explored on a boat ride/Mokoro boat ride. Get your camera ready, especially during sunset cruises!

  1. Top Botswana Tourism Attractions: Kalahari Desert

This is one of the unique attractions of Botswana that you shouldn’t miss out on while on your African safari tour in Botswana.

It’s a wonderful site for adventurous travelers interested in exploring weather in the desert as well as desert specialties like Meerkats, Cape Fox, Bat-eared Foxes, and Brown Hyenas. 3 African big cats (black-maned Kalahari Lions, Leopards, and Cheetahs), Zebras, Oryx, Wildebeests, Elands, Springboks, and Giraffes. Unique animal species, including porcupines and Honey badgers, can also be spotted on game drives on lucky days!

On the adventure in Kalahari, you will spot several beautiful bird species (over 220 birds recorded), like the Secretary bird, Martial Eagles, Giant Eagle Owls, Ostriches, White-headed Vultures, Lappet-faced Vultures, Black-breasted Snake Eagles, and yellow-billed Hornbill, among others.

Guided walking safaris or a walking safari with the San Bushmen in the Kalahari wilderness will leave you with memorable memories on your African safari in Botswana. The Bushmen will demonstrate to you their thrilling hunting skills and let you learn about their culture.

  1. Unique Attractions in Botswana: Makgadikgadi Salt Pans; Unique Wilderness of Botswana 

Amongst the beautiful things to see in Botswana is Makgadikgadi Salt Pans Park in the northeast of Botswana.

These unique salt pans are among the biggest salt pans on Earth, covering over 12,000km2. It’s one of the most common Botswana tourism attractions.

In summer, the desolate dry expanses of Makgadikgadi salt pans come to life with nutritious grasslands that attract various game animals like Cape Elephants, Giraffes, Baboons, Bushbucks, Springbok, Vervet Monkeys, Waterbucks, Zebras, Wildebeests, and Meerkats. Predators that reside in Makgadikgadi salt pans are Cheetahs, Spotted Hyenas, Lions, and Leopards.

During the rainy season, you can encounter numerous water birds and hundreds of game animals in the pans.

Safaris to Botswana are incomplete without a visit in Botswana Makgadikgadi Pans National Park. On your tour in this mystic land with desolate pans, you’ll enjoy breathtaking views of the surreal vast landscapes. Expect unusual sights!

Botswana tours to Makgadikgadi Salt Pans are possible all year. Visiting in the wet season offers you a chance to watch Southern Africa’s largest Zebra Migration from the Boteti River. Amazing big herds of Zebras will be witnessed moving through Botswana’s Makgadikgadi & Nxai Pan National Parks.

The above migration is the 2nd largest migration of wildlife in Africa after the Wildebeest migration in Serengeti and Masai Mara National Parks.

  1. Cultural Attractions of Botswana: Tsodilo Hills

Undeniably, Tsodilo Hills is among the top cultural tourism attractions of Botswana. This beautiful archaeological site on the edge of the jaw dropping Kalahari Desert in the northwest of Botswana was declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2001.

Tsodilo Hills is renowned for holding one of Africa’s densest collections of amazing rock paintings, depressions, shelters, and caves. On this site, over 4,500 paintings of the ancient Bushmen in an area of about 10km2 can be seen.

Expect to see several sites that depict hunting scenes, safari animals, and several ritual dances. Expeditions to this site are possible year-round.

The beautiful caves and shelters in these hills are believed to have existed for about 100,000 years ago. According to archaeologists, there are some rock paintings (art) that date back more than 20,000 years.

Currently, this area is an important religious sight to the local communities around it! Bushmen (the San) and the local community around believe that this was a sacred site for the first creation of man as well as a resting place for the spirits of the dead.

You will find a knowledgeable local guide about this site who will take you on the lovely nature walks in Tsodilo Hills.

  1. Unique Things to See in Botswana: The David Livingstone Memorial

David Livingstone Memorial is situated about 40km from Gaborone, where DR David once resided with his family.

This site comprises a church, the remnants of his house, a clinic and his family graveyard. It’s believed that, by the time Livingstone died, he had traveled about 800,000km within Africa for roughly 40 years.

At the time of his death, Livingstone is believed to have traveled over 80,000 kilometers through Africa overthe course of 40 years.

Note that Livingstone was a Scottish missionary who built Botswana’s first church, clinic, and school. It’s also one of the key man-made attractions of Botswana.

Best Time to Visit Botswana: Best Time to Go to Botswana—What is the Best Month to Visit in Botswana?

What is the best time to visit Botswana? Botswana safaris are rewarding all year round; however, we recommend our travelers interested in game drives undertake Botswana wildlife viewing tours in winter (dry months) from May to October. Normally, in these months, the temperatures are pleasant and it’s usually easy to spot game animals on water points during game drives.

The summer months (November–March) are usually wet and some points become inaccessible during game drives. This is the wet (green) season in this country.

  1. Why Is the Okavango Delta a Top Tourist Attraction in Botswana? 

Okavango Delta Botswana is prominent for its world-class game viewing tours (game drives), giving you a chance to spot the most sought-after species such as Cape Elephants, Buffalos, Giraffes, Lions, Leopards, Hippos, Crocodiles, and several Antelopes. This site is a paradise for bird watching in Botswana, as well as holding over 450 bird species.

  1. What Is the Cultural Attraction in Botswana?

The Tsodilo Hills are the major cultural tourist attractions of Botswana. It’s primarily known for its amazing ancient rock paintings (a spiritual outdoor art gallery), which date back over 100,000 years. This site holds the highest densities of Bushmen’s amazing rock art.
